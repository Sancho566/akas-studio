ZIOS

ZIOS is a conceptual luxury retail space imagined and brought to life by AKAS STUDIO. This flagship boutique blends the mystique of African heritage with the refined sophistication of Grecian aesthetics, creating a space where storytelling, craftsmanship, and sensuality converge. The interior is a symphony of soft curves, luminous textures, and sculptural symmetry. From the celestial blue vaulted ceiling to the radiant sunburst feature wall, every element invites admiration. Bold, modern lighting fixtures echo ancient star patterns, while curated materials celebrate natural beauty and timeless grandeur. Jewelry is presented like art in glass domes and asymmetrical display tables, turning each piece into a relic of desire. The palette, predominantly in warm neutrals with metallic accents, creates a serene yet opulent ambiance, allowing the product and space to harmonise effortlessly.
